# Care tracking glossary

## Child

A child profile shared by its members.

## Care session

One occurrence of care recorded at a specific time. A feeding is a care session, regardless of how many milk portions it contains.

## Feeding portion

A measured component of one feeding session. A portion has a milk type, delivery method, and amount. Several portions may belong to the same feeding session.

## Caregiver

A member who can record care in a child's shared care space.

## Viewer

A member who can view a child's shared care space but cannot record care.

## Owner

The member responsible for a child's shared care space and its membership.

## Child member

A person with access to one child's shared care space. A member's role belongs to that child, not to their account globally.
